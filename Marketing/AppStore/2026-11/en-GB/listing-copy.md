# Suggested App Store copy

## Subtitle

Tasks, habits & focused days

## Promotional text

Make room for what matters. Organise tasks, build habits and find your focus. All functionality is free. Premium is purely cosmetic and entirely optional.

## Description

Bring a little Clarity to your day.

Keep your tasks, habits and focus together in one calm place. Plan what matters, make time for it and see the progress you’re making.

PLAN YOUR DAY
Keep track of tasks and due dates. Organise your priorities with colourful categories, and set weekly targets that work for you.

BUILD YOUR HABITS
Set daily goals and log progress as you go. See your last seven days at a glance, with today always at the end of the row.

FIND YOUR FOCUS
Start a Pomodoro directly from a task. Choose a focused session from 5 to 25 minutes and give one thing your attention.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
Explore completed tasks, focus time and productivity patterns. Notice the small steps that add up over time.

ALL FUNCTIONALITY. FREE.
Every application feature is available for free. Clarity Premium is purely cosmetic and entirely optional: personalise your app icon and support development if you choose.
