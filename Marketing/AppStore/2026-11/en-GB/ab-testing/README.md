# Clarity — two creative treatments

A: Calm day — warm cream, sage shapes, task and habit UI. “Make space for your day.”
B: Full focus — indigo, yellow lightning and purple focus ring. “One task. Full focus.”

Each treatment includes:
- Header: 3840 × 1646 PNG
- Search: 3840 × 2560 PNG
- PPO opening screenshot: 1320 × 2868 PNG (iPhone)

All six exports are RGB with embedded sRGB profile and no transparency. Real app captures and the supplied logo are embedded in editable SVGs. No app code was changed. Nothing has been uploaded.

## Using the files

Apple documents Product Page Optimization for icons, screenshots and app previews; it does not list header/search creative assets as testable treatments. Use the two `ppo-screenshot` files as alternative first screenshots in PPO. Keep subsequent screenshots unchanged to compare these two opening concepts. These are concept tests: copy, colour and featured screen differ together, so results will not isolate a single design variable.

The `header` and `search` files are separate creative-placement artwork. Check them in App Store Connect’s placement Preview tool before submission. The principal artwork uses the same focal areas as the earlier Apple templates: header x1097 y493 width1646 height661; search x836 y765 width2168 height1030. Enlarged focal previews are in `previews/`; never upload those preview crops.

Suggested test: original page as control, plus A and B as two treatments. Choose the same English (UK) localization and keep other page assets consistent. Monitor conversion rate and Apple's confidence reporting before choosing a winner; neither design has measured performance yet.
